## Flower of Evil
**Flower of Evil** is a *South Korean thriller and melodrama* series that keeps you at the edge of your seat with every episode.
### Sypnosis
Baek Hee-sung appears to be the perfect husband and father, but he's actually living under a stolen identity. His wife, Cha Ji-won, is a detective who begins investigating a series of murders that resemble cases from the past.
### Thing I like about it
- Strong plot twists  
- Deep emotional story  
- Amazing acting and plot  
- Unique perspective on love and identity
### Themes
- Trust and deception   
- Justice and forgiveness
